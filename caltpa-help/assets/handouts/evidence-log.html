Before recording

□ Required permissions and approved storage confirmed
□ Teacher/student coverage and microphones tested in actual positions
□ Power, storage, lighting, sight lines, and safe cables checked
□ No unnecessary names, grades, or private records in view
□ Whole required lesson/unit/follow-up recording planned, not only predicted beats

After selection

□ Final clips within the current cycle/part limits
□ Picture and sound checked throughout; speakers distinguishable
□ Commentary timestamps refer to the final files
□ Missing/inaudible evidence is acknowledged, not reconstructed
□ Initial work and supported revisions are labeled separately
